Student Activities

Developing confidence, creativity, leadership and teamwork through learning beyond the classroom.

Beyond the Classroom

TG Model School provides students with opportunities to participate in academic, cultural, sports, creative and community-oriented activities.

Sports & Games

Opportunities for students to participate in athletics, indoor and outdoor games, physical education and sports events.

Cultural Activities

Cultural programmes, celebrations, music, dance, literary activities and events that encourage creativity and expression.

Science & Academic Activities

Science exhibitions, academic competitions, quizzes, projects and subject-based activities that promote curiosity.

Clubs & Competitions

Students are encouraged to take part in clubs, debates, essay writing, drawing, elocution and other competitions.

Community & Awareness

Awareness programmes, environmental activities, cleanliness drives and community-oriented initiatives.

Student Development

Activities focused on leadership, communication, teamwork, discipline, responsibility and overall personality development.
