Academics at TG Model School

TG Model Schools were established with the objective of providing quality education and creating opportunities for students to develop academically, personally and socially.

Our academic approach focuses on conceptual understanding, scientific thinking, communication skills, values, creativity and preparation for higher education.

School Education

Strong Foundations

Emphasis on fundamental concepts, subject knowledge, reading, writing, communication and analytical thinking.

Student-Centred Learning

Learning experiences encourage participation, questioning, discussion, exploration and application of knowledge.

Holistic Development

Academic learning is supported by activities that encourage creativity, confidence, values, discipline and leadership.

Future Readiness

Students are encouraged to develop the knowledge and skills required for higher education and future opportunities.

Junior College

The Junior College component provides students with an academic foundation for higher education and competitive opportunities.

  • Concept-based subject learning
  • Regular academic practice and assessment
  • Development of analytical and problem-solving skills
  • Guidance towards higher education
  • Encouragement of discipline and independent learning

Key Academic Focus Areas

Languages

Developing communication, reading, writing and comprehension skills.

Mathematics

Strengthening logical reasoning, numerical ability and problem-solving skills.

Science

Promoting scientific thinking, observation, experimentation and understanding of the natural world.

Social Studies

Understanding society, history, geography, economics, citizenship and democratic values.

Assessment & Examinations

Academic assessment helps teachers understand student progress and identify areas requiring additional support.

  • Classroom-based learning assessment
  • Periodic tests and examinations
  • Subject-wise academic performance review
  • Continuous feedback and improvement
  • Board and public examinations as applicable

Learning Beyond the Classroom

Education extends beyond textbooks. Academic learning is enriched through activities, projects, discussions, presentations, competitions, educational programmes and other learning experiences.

Our Academic Philosophy

Learn with understanding. Think independently. Apply knowledge. Grow with values.

The goal of education is not only academic achievement, but also the development of responsible, confident and capable young citizens.
