Intermediate – 1st & 2nd Year

Intermediate education is offered through MPC, BPC and CEC groups for First Year and Second Year students. Academic assessments and examinations are conducted according to the prescribed academic framework and official Board instructions.

Academic Approach

A balanced approach to classroom learning, assessment, co-curricular activities and preparation for higher education.

01

Classroom Learning

Structured classroom learning with emphasis on conceptual understanding, subject knowledge, active participation and continuous academic development.

02

Assessment

Regular assessments, examinations and academic activities help monitor student learning, identify learning needs and support continuous academic progress.

03

Co-curricular Activities

Co-curricular activities support creativity, communication, teamwork, leadership skills and the overall development of students.

04

Higher Education Preparation

Students receive academic guidance, higher education support and preparation for future studies and career opportunities.
