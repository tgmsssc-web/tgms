Assessment, Evaluation & Examination Information

Examinations at Our Institution

Examinations and assessments at TG Model School & Junior College are conducted in accordance with the applicable academic, assessment and examination guidelines issued by the concerned authorities of Telangana.

The examination framework differs between School Education and Intermediate Education. Accordingly, the information on this page is organised under the respective academic systems.

High School – Classes VI to X

School-level assessment and evaluation are carried out according to the applicable Telangana School Education and SCERT Telangana academic and assessment framework.

SCERT Telangana / School Education

Formative Assessment

Continuous assessment of student learning through classroom activities, written work, notebooks, projects, observation and other prescribed assessment tools.

Summative Assessment

Assessment of learning against the prescribed curriculum, competencies and learning outcomes at appropriate stages of the academic year.

Classroom & Periodic Assessment

Regular academic assessment helps teachers identify learning progress and provide appropriate support to students.

Academic Records

Student assessment records are maintained as required under the applicable School Education instructions.

SSC Public Examination – Class X

The Class X SSC Public Examination is conducted under the Directorate of Government Examinations, Telangana, in accordance with the official examination notifications, rules and instructions issued from time to time.

SSC Examination Information
  • Official examination timetable and instructions will be displayed when issued.
  • Students should carefully follow hall-ticket and examination-centre instructions.
  • Students must follow the prescribed examination discipline and rules.
  • Only officially issued information will be treated as authoritative.

Intermediate – 1st & 2nd Year

Intermediate education is offered through MPC, BPC and CEC groups for First Year and Second Year students. Academic assessments and examinations are conducted according to the prescribed academic framework and official Board instructions.

Telangana Board of Intermediate Education

1st Year Examinations

Academic and public examination information for Intermediate First Year students will be published according to official Board instructions.

2nd Year Examinations

Examination schedules, instructions and related information will follow the latest official Board notifications.

Theory Examinations

Theory examinations will be conducted according to the prescribed syllabus, timetable and examination regulations.

Practical Examinations

Practical examinations will be conducted according to the applicable Board guidelines and schedules.

General Examination Instructions

  • Students should report to the examination venue on time.
  • Students should carry the required hall ticket or examination identity document.
  • Students must follow the instructions given by the examination authorities and invigilators.
  • Students should bring only permitted materials into the examination hall.
  • Electronic devices and other prohibited items must not be brought into the examination hall.
  • Students should maintain discipline and follow examination-centre procedures.
  • Any examination-specific instruction issued by the competent authority will prevail over general information on this page.
