About TG Model School & Junior College – Dandepally

Telangana Model Schools were established to provide quality education in educationally backward areas and to create strong educational opportunities for children, particularly in rural communities.

The Model School programme was designed to provide quality English-medium education under the State syllabus from Class VI through Intermediate, while giving importance to curricular and co-curricular development.

The official Model School objectives emphasise holistic education, communication skills, student-centred learning, moral and ethical values, scientific and rational thinking, leadership and the development of each child's potential.

Why Model Schools Were Established

The Model School initiative originated as a Government of India programme for Educationally Backward Blocks (EBBs), with the objective of providing free quality education and creating schools that could play a pace-setting role in their areas.

In Telangana, Model Schools were developed as institutions intended to bring quality educational opportunities closer to students in rural and educationally backward areas.

The programme also seeks to develop schools as centres of excellence and to provide an environment in which students can grow academically, socially, physically and morally.

Aim of Model Schools

The documented aim of Model Schools is to develop the character and individuality of students and to nurture their intellectual, personal and human potential.

Quality Education

Centre of Excellence

Our Educational Philosophy

The Model School philosophy recognises that every child is a unique individual with diverse talents and potential. Education therefore goes beyond examination performance and aims to prepare students for life.

  • Nurturing the intellectual abilities of every child.
  • Developing scientific and rational thinking.
  • Building self-confidence, responsibility and discipline.
  • Developing leadership qualities and communication skills.
  • Promoting moral and ethical values.
  • Encouraging learning through curricular and co-curricular experiences.
  • Supporting all-round personality and character development.
  • Preparing students to respond to changing knowledge, technology and society.

Our Vision

To provide high-quality education that helps students develop knowledge, skills, values, confidence and responsibility, enabling them to become capable and responsible citizens.

Our Mission

To create a student-centred learning environment that combines academic excellence with character formation, scientific thinking, creativity, communication, leadership, sports and co-curricular development.
